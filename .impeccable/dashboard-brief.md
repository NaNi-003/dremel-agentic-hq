# Predictive Trend Engine Dashboard

Mode: Operate, with Monitor as the primary information posture.

Audience: Dremel marketing stakeholders reviewing opportunities and preparing campaigns.
Task: scan ranked signals, inspect evidence, open any primary Maya brief, and model marketing ROI.
Constraints: preserve every working behavior, deterministic ordering, top-15 initial display, progressive load-more, Dremel colors, and static deployment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Marketing Intelligence Console organizes the overall dashboard for rapid scanning and confident comparison; it refuses a promotional hero and generic equal-weight card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Dremel blue anchors navigation and decision controls, Dremel orange marks priority and momentum, and clean white work surfaces use crisp rules, compact radii, and disciplined data typography.

STORY: The user sees the strongest market signals, moves between analysis views, selects a primary opportunity, opens its complete campaign brief, and tests its marketing economics without leaving the workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact branded rail sits beside a wide intelligence workspace. The title and four concise metrics establish state without technical provenance. Navigation is immediately visible. Autonomous Content Ideation becomes a more visual Campaign Opportunity Studio with opportunity selection, an editorial brief canvas, and a clear brief action.

FORM: One unified production dashboard: Marketing Intelligence Console across the product, with Campaign Opportunity Studio as the visual and brief-focused ideation section. User-pinned direction; no concept tournament required.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
