# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Dremel marketing stakeholders evaluating evidence-backed YouTube campaign opportunities for the UK market.

## Product Purpose

Turn collected YouTube evidence into ranked marketing opportunities, candidate-specific content briefs, and campaign ROI scenarios suitable for planning and demonstration.

## Positioning

The product combines deterministic opportunity ranking with auditable evidence. An analyst approves candidates, and creator briefs are saved only for those approvals.

## Operating Context

Users first scan portfolio-level opportunity signals, then inspect individual trends, content briefs, source evidence, and marketing ROI assumptions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve deterministic ranking and expose the top 15 opportunities first.
- Reveal secondary candidates progressively in groups of ten.
- Show creator briefs for candidates an analyst has approved.
- Preserve the original unconstrained ROI and add availability-constrained ROI only as a marketing scenario.
- CV-1 remains thumbnail-only; CV-2 is out of scope.
- Keep implementation static and compatible with here.now publication.
- Keep technical provenance in artifacts rather than the executive interface.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Dremel's established blue-and-orange color identity and supplied Dremel logo. The interface should feel professional, precise, useful, and recognizably Dremel rather than like a generic AI dashboard.

## Evidence on Hand

The published dashboard payload contains ranked opportunity rows, source thumbnails and URLs, viewer sentiment, thumbnail evidence when available, run counts, ROI inputs, and fifteen candidate-bound Maya briefs.

## Product Principles

- Marketing decisions before technical process detail.
- Evidence remains inspectable.
- Primary opportunities remain immediately understandable.
- Advanced detail appears through progressive disclosure.
- Keep the workflow simple enough for a reliable live demonstration.
