# Dremel Marketing Intelligence Design System

## Direction

A unified Marketing Intelligence Console optimized for scanning, comparison, evidence inspection, and campaign modeling. Autonomous Content Ideation uses a more visual Campaign Opportunity Studio treatment while remaining part of the same product system.

## Color tokens

- `--dremel-blue: #005b9f` — primary brand and interactive color.
- `--dremel-blue-dark: #003f70` — navigation, headings, and high-emphasis surfaces.
- `--dremel-blue-soft: #eaf4fa` — low-emphasis informational surfaces.
- `--dremel-orange: #c64d0d` — accessible Dremel-orange interaction and momentum accent.
- `--dremel-orange-dark: #9f3905` — orange text and hover state.
- `--ink: #17212b` — primary body text.
- `--muted: #5b6875` — supporting text.
- `--line: #d9e1e8` — standard dividers.
- `--canvas: #f4f7f9` and `--surface: #ffffff` — workspace layers.

Dremel orange is a signal, not a large surface. Dremel blue carries product identity and navigation.

## Typography

Use Source Sans 3 when available, followed by Segoe UI and Arial. Headings are compact, bold, and blue. Data uses tabular numerals. Small operational labels may use restrained uppercase tracking; body copy remains sentence case.

## Shape and depth

- Controls and action buttons use 4px radii.
- Image thumbnails use 2px radii.
- Data groups favor shared borders and one-pixel separators over independent floating cards.
- Shadows are restrained and reserved for complete work surfaces.

## Layout

- Desktop: fixed blue strategy rail plus broad intelligence workspace.
- Tablet: strategy controls become a full-width top region.
- Mobile: all content becomes a single column with horizontal section navigation.
- The primary dashboard posture is dense and glanceable rather than promotional.

## Components

### Intelligence metrics

Metrics share one continuous ruled surface. Labels are compact; values are prominent and use tabular numerals.

### Navigation

Section tabs use a simple orange active rule. Labels contain no decorative emoji.

### Campaign Opportunity Studio

A blue guidance rail explains the two-step workflow. The selected opportunity occupies a large editorial canvas, with an orange action leading to the complete Maya brief.

### Tables and charts

Tables use compact rows, sticky headers, source links, thumbnail evidence, and progressive disclosure. Charts stay on white analytical surfaces.

### ROI workspace

Inputs and results share one divided workspace. Marketing availability remains a scenario guardrail and does not introduce operational or stock-management framing.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Minimum control height is 44px.
- Keyboard focus uses a visible orange ring.
- Motion is limited to a short panel transition and is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Important text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast using accessible darker shades of the Dremel palette.
- Working behavior, deterministic ranking, top-15 initial feed, load-more increments, and all primary Maya briefs must remain intact.
